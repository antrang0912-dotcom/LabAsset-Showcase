About the project

LabAsset — Smart Lab Management

Computer Lab Asset and Preventive Maintenance Management System.

LabAsset is designed to organise computer laboratory assets, support preventive maintenance workflows and provide a clear record of lab operations.
♙

Role-based access

Separate application roles for Admin, Lab In-charge and Technician.

▤

Asset management

Register and manage computer assets, lab assignments and asset details.

◷

Preventive maintenance

Plan maintenance, assign tasks and record task completion.

▦

QR code support

Generate QR codes to help identify and access asset records.

▧

Document management

Store supporting documents such as warranty files using cloud object storage.

▥

Reports & audit trail

Review activity logs, maintenance summaries and analytics reports.
